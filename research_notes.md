## 1. Ogden, UT Stop

### Pick: Union Station (Ogden Union Station)

**Address:** 2501 Wall Avenue, Ogden, UT 84401
**Coordinates:** 41.22083° N, 111.97972° W (41°13′15″N 111°58′47″W)
Source: [Wikipedia — Union Station (Ogden, Utah)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_Station_(Ogden,_Utah))

**Popup description (for map, ~35–55 words):**
> **Ogden — Union Station.** This 1924 Spanish Colonial Revival depot anchors historic 25th Street, once one of the West's busiest rail junctions. Today it houses railroad, firearms, and classic-car museums under grand arched windows — a quick, walkable stop with easy access to Ogden's best restaurants and shops. (44 words)

**Longer note (printed stop list, 2–4 sentences):**
> Ogden's Union Station has stood at the west end of Historic 25th Street since 1924, replacing an 1889 depot destroyed by fire — it's actually the third station on this site since the original 1869 wooden building welcomed the Transcontinental Railroad ([Deseret News](https://www.deseret.com/utah/2024/11/21/deseret-news-archives-ogden-union-station-opens-in-1924-to-great-fanfare/)). Designed by father-son architects John and Donald Parkinson (who later built Los Angeles's Union Station), the building once handled up to 120 trains a day carrying presidents, movie stars, and WWII servicemen bound for the Pacific ([Visit Ogden](https://www.visitogden.com/blog/all-thanks-to-frank-celebrating-100-years-of-ogdens-union-station/), [HMDB historical marker](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=277896)). Passenger rail ended in 1997, and the depot now serves as home to the Utah State Railroad Museum, the John M. Browning Firearms Museum, and the Browning-Kimball Classic Car Museum ([Ogden City](https://www.ogdencity.gov/1329/Museums)). It celebrated its 100th anniversary in November 2024 with the opening of a century-old time capsule sealed in its cornerstone ([KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/51030147/100-year-old-time-capsule-at-ogdens-union-station-yields-train-timetables-newspapers-pictures)).

## 2. Dining Options by Town

### Lava Hot Springs, ID
*(No cityweekly.net coverage found for this Idaho town — options sourced from local/tourism sites.)*
1. **78 Main Street Eatery** — Classic American comfort food (burgers, sandwiches, hearty breakfasts) with indoor/outdoor seating and a true small-town vibe; 357+ reviews on Tripadvisor. ([Lava Hot Springs dining guide](https://lavahotsprings.org/cat/dining-nightlife/); [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurants-g35511-Lava_Hot_Springs_Idaho.html))
2. **Portneuf Grille & Lounge** (at Riverside Hot Springs Inn) — Northwest-inspired fine dining featuring fresh seafood and USDA-aged prime steak; reservations recommended. ([idahohotsprings.com](https://www.idahohotsprings.com/destinations/lava-hot-springs/lava-hot-springs.htm))
3. **Riverwalk Thai** — Authentic, family-owned Thai restaurant in a converted former gas station near the hot springs; casual lunch/dinner spot open Tuesday–Sunday. ([idahohotsprings.com](https://www.idahohotsprings.com/destinations/lava-hot-springs/lava-hot-springs.htm); [lavacampground.com](https://www.lavacampground.com/dining.html))

### Soda Springs, ID
*(No cityweekly.net coverage found — options sourced from Tripadvisor/general web, verified as currently operating.)*
1. **Caribou Jack's Trading Co & BBQ** — A retail/hardware store with an in-house BBQ café serving Traeger-smoked meats and home-cooked sides; located at 50 W 2nd S. ([Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g35606-d14131363-Reviews-Caribou_Jack_s_Trading_Co_BBQ-Soda_Springs_Idaho.html))
2. **Arctic Circle Restaurant** — Regional fast-food chain outpost, a reliable quick stop for burgers and shakes. ([Tripadvisor rankings](https://no.tripadvisor.com/Restaurants-g35606-zfg16556-Soda_Springs_Idaho.html))
3. **Enders Hotel & Restaurant** — Historic hotel dining room in downtown Soda Springs known for classic American comfort food (verify current hours locally, as small-town hours can shift seasonally).

### Logan, UT
1. **Le Nonne Ristorante Italiano** (129 N 100 East) — Widely regarded as Logan's best restaurant; northern Italian menu heavy on homemade pastas like crab-and-ricotta ravioli. Cited by City Weekly as "not just Logan's best restaurant but one of the best in the Beehive State." ([cityweekly.net](https://www.cityweekly.net/utah/second-to-nonne-from-northern-italy-to-northern-utah-thereandrsquos-no-better-gnocchi-than-micheliandrsquos/Content?oid=2128297))
2. **Elements Restaurant** (35 E 640 South, Riverwoods development) — New American fine dining along the Logan River with a scenic riverside patio. ([cityweekly.net](https://www.cityweekly.net/utah/elements-fine-dining-in-logan/Content?oid=2153126))
3. **Cafe Sabor** (600 W Center St) — Tex-Mex fusion served inside a charming historic train depot; a Logan institution for 15+ years with locals' specials on Thursdays. ([onlyinyourstate.com](https://www.onlyinyourstate.com/food/utah/restaurant-that-used-to-be-something-else-ut); not a cityweekly.net find — sourced from general web)

### Ogden, UT
1. **Hearth on 25th** (195 25th St) — Longtime 25th Street favorite (formerly Jasoh!), rebranded as Hearth in 2013; New American menu with the Title 32B bar attached. ([cityweekly.net](https://www.cityweekly.net/utah/hearth/Content?oid=3027284))
2. **Slackwater Pub & Pizzeria** (1895 Washington Blvd / also 209 24th St location) — Popular gastropub known for wood-fired pizza and a large patio. ([cityweekly.net](https://www.cityweekly.net/utah/slackwater-pub-and-pizzeria/Content?oid=2158777))
3. **La Crepe OG (La Crepe Og Gourmet)** (2401/2411 Kiesel Ave) — Ogden eatery known for sweet and savory crepes and elaborate "Freak Shakes"; located in a picturesque downtown alleyway. ([cityweekly.net](https://www.cityweekly.net/utah/the-thrill-chill-cult/Content?oid=16969345))
