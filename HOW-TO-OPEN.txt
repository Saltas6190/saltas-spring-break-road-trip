HOW TO OPEN THIS MAP
=====================

This ZIP file contains one file: "Murray to Lava Hot Springs Road Trip.html"
It is the full interactive map (pins, popups, photos) — it just needs to be
unzipped and opened in a web browser, not an email or file-preview app.

ON AN IPHONE / IPAD
1. Tap the attachment to download the ZIP, then tap it again to unzip it
   (Files app will do this automatically).
2. Find "Murray to Lava Hot Springs Road Trip.html" in the Files app.
3. Tap and hold the file, choose "Share," then choose "Safari" (or any
   browser icon) from the share sheet. If Safari isn't listed, tap "Open in..."
   and pick a browser.

ON AN ANDROID PHONE
1. Tap the attachment to download the ZIP. Most phones unzip it automatically
   into your Downloads or Files app — if not, tap the ZIP to extract it.
2. Open the Files app, find "Murray to Lava Hot Springs Road Trip.html."
3. Tap it, and if asked which app to open it with, choose Chrome (or any
   browser).

ON A COMPUTER (WINDOWS OR MAC)
1. Download the ZIP and double-click it to unzip.
2. Double-click "Murray to Lava Hot Springs Road Trip.html."
   It should open automatically in your default web browser.
   If it opens as a page of code instead of the map, right-click the file,
   choose "Open with," and pick Chrome, Safari, or Edge.

WHY THIS MATTERS
Some email apps try to "preview" HTML files directly inside the email and
block the interactive parts for safety, which can make the map look blank
or broken. Unzipping the file first and opening it directly in a real
browser avoids that problem entirely. No login, no account, and no
internet connection needed once it's downloaded — everything (map tiles
need internet, but all photos and pins) are built into the single file.
